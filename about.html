What Nothing Else Does

Three things you will not find anywhere else in Indian HR tech.

Flagship · Entry Point
⚡ ER Case Navigator
Every ER situation gets two layers, always, no exceptions. The PACT diagnostic scans Process Integrity, Accountability Clarity, Consequence Mapping, and Trust Preservation in under a minute. Then the seven-section Decision Protocol gives you the legal position, the exact financial exposure in INR, the MERIDIAN-calibrated context, and the specific language to use — with the stakeholder you're about to talk to, not a generic script.
Open ER Case Navigator →
Flagship · Retention Decisions
⚖️ Offer Intelligence
The counter-offer decision that used to be a gut call now has a break-even ceiling attached to it. Two flows: a live resignation with a competing offer in hand, or a proactive flight-risk read before anything's been said out loud. Either way, you get the replacement cost, the retention cost at three match levels, a flagged primary recommendation, and two scripts — one for the BU Head, one for the employee.
Open Offer Intelligence →
Flagship · The Career Record
📜 The Ledger
HR gets overruled and then gets blamed six months later when the overruled path fails. The Ledger flips that. File your position, the financial basis, and your prediction before the decision goes the other way. Six weeks or six months on, close the loop and see who the outcome actually vindicated. Not a confrontation. A timestamped professional record that speaks for itself.
