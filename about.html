1. SCOPE

The employment relationship,
in its full complexity.

The moment someone joins your organisation until the moment they leave: every decision in between that carries legal exposure, financial consequence, or stakeholder risk. That is the zone Vantage operates in.

Most HR tools try to cover all of HR. Vantage doesn't. It covers the part that requires the most professional judgment, carries the highest financial and legal stakes, and produces, when handled well, the kind of evidence that changes how an HR professional is perceived in any room they walk into.

That part is the employment relationship. The live, ongoing relationship between an employer and the people already inside the organisation. Every disciplinary process. Every at-risk conversation. Every moment where the line between a good outcome and an expensive tribunal starts to matter.

2. THE TOOLS

Four tools and ARIA.
Each one built for a specific moment.

Each tool in Vantage is its own structured intelligence engine: its own diagnostic frameworks, its own financial models, all calibrated through MERIDIAN to your specific professional context. ARIA is different in kind. She thinks with you. The tools think for the situation.

Employee Case Advisor
USE WHEN: You have an ER situation with legal stakes
A manager wants to terminate a 5-year employee with no written warning on file. You describe the situation. The Case Advisor runs the PACT diagnostic (process gaps, accountability clarity, consequence mapping, trust impact) and gives you a structured reading of the process, the financial exposure range in INR, what documentation you need today, and the recommended path forward, not a legal determination. Not a template. A structured decision.
The Business Case
USE WHEN: Numbers need to become an argument, for a room or for one person
Your attrition jumped from 14% to 22% in one quarter. If you need ₹22L approved by the wider leadership team, The Business Case converts your metrics into a CFO-grade narrative: revenue at risk, cost per departure, three audience-specific versions, boardroom, CFO, CEO. If it comes down to one person instead, your VP Engineering, who needs to approve a counter-offer before Friday and responds only to financial risk, it builds the five-layer influence stack instead: credibility anchor, data anchor, narrative bridge, risk mirror, decision runway, calibrated to her specific risk priorities. You walk in with a modelled rupee figure, not an HR report, whoever is in the room.
Policy Advisor
USE WHEN: The policy is unclear or you're not sure what applies
A manager asks if he can extend a fixed-term contract employee's probation. You don't know if your Standing Orders cover it. The Policy Advisor gives you the applicable law, the specific section, what your state's rules say, and the recommended path, in plain language. The provisions that appear to apply, with the section cited. Not a substitute for counsel.
Retention Advisor
USE WHEN: Someone might leave and the cost isn't clear yet
Your senior data architect just got a 35% offer from a competitor. She's been here four years and owns half the platform's institutional knowledge. The Retention Advisor models the full replacement cost (recruitment, ramp time, knowledge transfer) against the cost of matching or exceeding the offer, and gives you the break-even ceiling, the recommended position, and draft language for both conversations. Walk in knowing your number before they ask for theirs.
ARIA: Your HR Intelligence Companion
DIFFERENT IN KIND · NOT A STRUCTURED TOOL. A THINKING PARTNER.
The four tools above give you structured outputs for specific situations. ARIA is the one you talk to when the situation is still messy. When you don't know which tool to reach for. When you're processing a difficult week. When you want to think out loud at 9pm and have someone who understands the Indian HR professional reality ask you the question you hadn't thought to ask yourself. She doesn't produce a formatted output. She listens, reflects, and moves your thinking forward, one question at a time. The tools think for the situation. ARIA thinks with you.

5. HR DECISION CAPITAL

The number your HR career
has never been able to produce.

Every HR professional knows what they've done. Vantage writes the planning figure down, with the assumption attached. It does not prove the rupee value.

When you navigate a termination risk that carried a ₹14L tribunal exposure and handled it correctly, that's documented. When you retained someone whose departure would have cost ₹34L to replace, that's documented. When you landed the ₹22L retention programme business case in a room full of CFO scepticism, that's documented.

The record is the sum of the cases. Each exposure is a model, not a verified saving. The cumulative figure is a running log of those models, not capital created.

It is not your salary. It is not your job title. It is the documented financial impact of your professional judgment, specific to the employment relationship, where that judgment is most exposed and most consequential.

It is built from three components, each wired in as you use the tools that produce them.

The first, Financial Exposure Navigated, is live from your very first Employee Case Advisor session. Every case you navigate generates a documented financial exposure in INR, and every case adds to your running total automatically, in your Vantage Record.

The second, Recorded replacement cost, accumulates from every flight risk decision you model through Retention Advisor: the replacement cost modelled, the counter-offer ceiling you calculated, the decision you made with numbers attached.

The third, Recorded business-case figures, accumulates from every financial case you build using The Business Case: the Revenue at Risk figure you surfaced, the rupee argument you walked into the room with, the exposure you made visible. Whether it was approved is tracked separately; what is documented here is the professional case you made and the financial stakes you quantified.

A first year on Vantage builds a number. Three years builds a career argument. Five years builds something no other HR professional in India can produce: a financially quantified, timestamped, defensible record of what your professional practice is actually worth.

At your next job interview, walk in with this number. Most HR professionals bring opinions. You bring a timestamped log of the models.

6. QUANTIFY · powered by Humac Score

A second number.
Measuring something different.

HR Decision Capital and the Humac Score are not the same thing, and the Humac Score isn't a renamed version of it. Here's the line between them, stated plainly before anything else in this section.

HR DECISION CAPITAL A cumulative rupee figure. It only grows. It's the running total of the financial exposure, replacement cost, and business-case figures your decisions have documented, across every case you've worked. It's about you and your professional track record.
HUMAC SCORE A score out of 100, recalculated from where things stand right now across five pillars. It can go up or down. It's about your organisation's current people-financial health, not your individual record.

Five pillars feed the score: Value Ledger (modelled return per rupee invested in people), Talent Premium (performance above market for the pay you're giving), Org Vitals (an organisational health index), Human P&L (modelled margin), and Human Balance Sheet (total human capital position).

The Humac Score is a provisional index, currently version 0.9. It's built to run on all five pillars, but what you see is computed only from the pillars your case history actually supports. Pillars without enough data yet are marked out, not estimated to make the number look complete.

It exists because HR has never had a single number the boardroom already trusts the way a revenue or EBITDA figure is trusted. The Humac Score is Vantage's attempt at building that number, not a finished claim that it already is one.
