The intelligence layer
HR has always needed.

Built for Indian HR professionals who are done justifying their existence — and ready to hold leadership financially accountable for the people decisions they make.

The problem

HR has a credibility gap. Not a capability gap.

HR professionals in India are technically capable, deeply experienced, and often the most ethically serious people in the organisation. But they keep getting overruled, underfunded, and ignored — not because they're wrong, but because they're speaking the wrong language.

The language of the boardroom is financial. Decisions that get funded come with a number. Departments that earn respect hold others accountable. HR has been waiting for a seat at the table by speaking the language of care. Vantage is how you earn it by speaking the language of capital.

What Vantage is

HR Decision Intelligence. Not a dashboard. Not a chatbot.

Vantage is not an HRMS. It is not a productivity suite. It is not a dashboard that reports what happened last quarter so you can present it to someone who doesn't care.

Vantage is an intelligence layer. It takes every ER case, every people decision, every difficult conversation with a resistant stakeholder — and translates it into the financial consequence the business needs to hear. The legal position it needs to respect. The recommendation it can act on today.

When you walk into a room with Vantage behind you, you walk in with numbers. Not opinions. Not intuitions. Numbers that the CFO, CEO, and board understand — because they're in the language they already think in.

The calibration engine

MERIDIAN: why your answers are different from everyone else's.

Every answer Vantage gives you is calibrated to your exact professional reality through a 7-parameter profile called MERIDIAN. Generic HR advice is how you get generic HR outcomes. MERIDIAN is how you stop getting both.

A POSH compliance question for a 60-person startup in Bengaluru is a different question from the same issue at a 2,000-person manufacturing unit in Nashik with certified standing orders and an active union. Vantage knows the difference — because MERIDIAN tells it.

The financial framework

Humac: the score that measures what people investment actually returns.

Vantage measures the financial efficiency of your people investment through a proprietary framework called Humac. Five pillars. One composite score. A Trajectory Index that shows where you're heading, not just where you are.

The Humac Score is not an engagement score. It is not an eNPS. It is a financial output — denominated in the same currency the CFO uses, aligned with ISO 30414 Human Capital Reporting Standards, and designed to appear in board decks, not HR reports.

L1
Value Ledger
The total financial cost and return of attrition, hiring, retention, and talent acquisition. Every exit and every hire has a number — this is where it lives.
L2
Talent Premium
The return on your Stars and HiPos above the performance baseline — and the liability of your Inconsistent Performers below it. Performance has a financial premium.
L3
Org Vitals
Twenty organisational telemetry signals across Leadership, Fear, Middle Layer, Execution, and HR Mirror. The diagnostic dimension that predicts where the organisation is heading.
L4
Human P&L
HR programme costs, compliance spend, legal exposure, and below-the-line adjustments. The costs that never appear in the P&L but are always paid somewhere.
L5
Human Balance Sheet
Institutional knowledge concentration, succession depth, and talent departure risk. The assets that don't appear on any financial statement but determine whether the organisation can execute.
Built for India

Not adapted for India. Built in it.

Every legal citation in Vantage uses BNS, BNSS, and BSA — the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam — the codes that replaced IPC, CrPC, and the Indian Evidence Act on 1 July 2024. Vantage does not cite repealed statutes. It does not offer generic South Asian defaults. Every answer knows whether you're in Maharashtra or Telangana, whether you're in manufacturing or fintech, whether your standing orders are model or certified.

Every financial calculation is in INR. Every benchmark reflects Indian private sector practice. Every POSH citation knows the difference between the Internal Complaints Committee and what it is often incorrectly called.

The vision

HR holding leadership accountable — not the other way around.

The dominant model of HR justifying itself to the business — seeking approval, presenting business cases, waiting for budget — assumes HR is a support function. Vantage is built on a different premise: that HR is the function that sits on the most consequential financial lever in any organisation, and that the reason it has been treated as a cost centre is not because it is one, but because it has not had the tools to prove otherwise.

The vision for Vantage is specific: every HR professional in India should be able to walk into a boardroom, a difficult performance conversation, or a complex disciplinary situation — and have the intelligence, the legal grounding, and the financial evidence to lead the decision, not just implement it.
