01 / SCOPE

The employment relationship —
in its full complexity.

The moment someone joins your organisation until the moment they leave — every decision in between that carries legal exposure, financial consequence, or stakeholder risk. That is the zone Vantage operates in.

Most HR tools try to cover all of HR. Vantage doesn't. It covers the part that requires the most professional judgment, carries the highest financial and legal stakes, and produces — when handled well — the kind of evidence that changes how an HR professional is perceived in any room they walk into.

That part is the employment relationship. The live, ongoing relationship between an employer and the people already inside the organisation. Every disciplinary process. Every at-risk conversation. Every moment where the line between a good outcome and an expensive tribunal starts to matter.

Vantage doesn't touch talent acquisition — that's the pipeline before someone joins. It doesn't touch L&D programme design — that's the curriculum after they're settled. It doesn't do payroll, HRIS configuration, or compensation benchmarking. These are deliberate choices, not limitations. The employment relationship is complex enough to deserve a platform built entirely around it.

02 / THE TOOLS

Six tools and ARIA.
Each one built for a specific moment.

Each tool in Vantage is its own structured intelligence engine — its own frameworks, its own financial models, its own legal calibration. ARIA is different in kind. She thinks with you. The tools think for the situation.

⚡
Employee Case Advisor
USE WHEN: You have an ER situation with legal stakes
A manager wants to terminate a 5-year employee with no written warning on file. You describe the situation. The Case Advisor runs the PACT diagnostic — process gaps, accountability clarity, consequence mapping, trust impact — and gives you the exact legal position, the financial exposure in INR, what documentation you need today, and the recommended path forward. Not a template. A structured decision.
📊
People ROI Brief
USE WHEN: You have HR numbers that need to become a business argument
Your attrition jumped from 14% to 22% in one quarter. You need ₹22L approved for a retention programme. The People ROI Brief converts your metrics into a CFO-grade financial narrative — revenue at risk, cost per departure, sector benchmark comparison, and three audience-specific versions: boardroom, CFO, CEO. You walk in with a rupee figure, not an HR report.
🎯
Leadership Communicator
USE WHEN: You need a specific person to take a specific action
You need your VP Engineering to approve a counter-offer before Friday. She responds to financial risk, not people narratives. The Leadership Communicator builds the five-layer influence stack — credibility anchor, data anchor, narrative bridge, risk mirror, decision runway — calibrated to how that specific stakeholder thinks. Opening line, closing line, and everything in between.
🎭
Difficult Conversations
USE WHEN: The conversation tomorrow feels high-stakes
You have to tell a line manager that his star performer is leaving and he contributed to it. ARIA plays the manager — resistant, defensive, not ready to hear it. You practice the conversation until the argument lands cleanly and you've heard every form of pushback before the real meeting. Not a script. A rehearsal that makes the real thing easier to navigate.
🧭
Policy Advisor
USE WHEN: The policy is unclear or you're not sure what applies
A manager asks if he can extend a fixed-term contract employee's probation. You don't know if your Standing Orders cover it. The Policy Advisor gives you the applicable law, the specific section, what your state's rules say, and the recommended path — in plain language, without needing a lawyer on speed dial. Three paragraphs. Cited. Actionable today.
⚖️
Retention Advisor
USE WHEN: Someone might leave and the cost isn't clear yet
Your senior data architect just got a 35% offer from a competitor. She's been here four years and owns half the platform's institutional knowledge. The Retention Advisor models the full replacement cost — recruitment, ramp time, knowledge transfer — against the cost of matching or exceeding the offer, and gives you the break-even ceiling, the recommended position, and the exact language for both conversations. Walk in knowing your number before they ask for theirs.
✶
ARIA — Your HR Intelligence Companion
DIFFERENT IN KIND — NOT A STRUCTURED TOOL. A THINKING PARTNER.
The six tools above give you structured outputs for specific situations. ARIA is the one you talk to when the situation is still messy. When you don't know which tool to reach for. When you're processing a difficult week. When you want to think out loud at 9pm and have someone who understands the Indian HR professional reality ask you the question you hadn't thought to ask yourself. She doesn't produce a formatted output. She listens, reflects, and moves your thinking forward — one question at a time. The tools think for the situation. ARIA thinks with you.
03 / ARIA

ARIA does not power
the other tools.

This is one of the most common questions we get. The answer matters because it changes how you use Vantage.

Each Vantage tool — the Employee Case Advisor, the People ROI Brief, the Policy Advisor, all of them — is its own structured intelligence engine. Each has its own diagnostic framework, its own financial model, its own legal calibration specific to Indian employment law. When you use the Employee Case Advisor, you are not having a conversation with ARIA. You are running a structured protocol that produces a formatted output every time.

ARIA is different in kind, not just in name. She is your conversational professional companion. The one you talk to when the situation is too messy for a structured tool. When you don't know what you're dealing with yet. When you're at 9pm and the call tomorrow morning is in your head and you need to think, not process.

The tools give you answers. ARIA helps you figure out what you're actually asking.

04 / HR DECISION CAPITAL

The number your HR career
has never been able to produce.

Every HR professional knows what they've done. Almost none of them can prove it — in rupees, with evidence attached.

When you navigate a termination risk that carried a ₹14L tribunal exposure and handled it correctly — that's documented. When you retained someone whose departure would have cost ₹34L to replace — that's documented. When you landed the ₹22L retention programme business case in a room full of CFO scepticism — that's documented.

HR Decision Capital is the sum of all of it. Every case you navigated on Vantage, every financial exposure you handled, every business case you landed — converted into a single cumulative figure that tells the financial story of your HR practice.

It is not your salary. It is not your job title. It is the documented financial impact of your professional judgment — specific to the employment relationship, where that judgment is most exposed and most consequential.

It is computed from three components:

Financial exposure navigated — the INR ranges from every PACT case, at midpoint. Retention value protected — the replacement cost avoided from every flight risk you managed. Business cases landed — the programmes approved because of your People ROI Brief.

A first year on Vantage builds a number. Three years builds a career argument. Five years builds something no other HR professional in India can produce — a financially quantified, timestamped, defensible record of what your professional practice is actually worth.

At your next job interview, walk in with this number. Most HR professionals bring opinions. You bring receipts.
