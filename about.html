The Full Platform

Six tools. One integrated intelligence system.

Each tool addresses a specific moment in the HR professional's work. Together they form a complete decision loop.

The Problem

HR has a credibility gap. Not a capability gap.

HR professionals in India are technically capable, deeply experienced, and often the most ethically serious people in the organisation. But they keep getting overruled, underfunded, and ignored — not because they're wrong, but because they're speaking the wrong language.

The language of the boardroom is financial. Decisions that get funded come with a number. Departments that earn respect hold others accountable. Vantage is built on a different premise: HR is the function that sits on the most consequential financial lever in any organisation. The reason it has been treated as a cost centre is not because it is one — but because it hasn't had the tools to prove otherwise.

The Calibration Engine

The Financial Framework

Humac: the score that measures what people investment actually returns.

Five pillars. One composite score. A Trajectory Index that shows where you're heading, not just where you are. The Humac Score is denominated in the same currency the CFO uses — designed to appear in board decks, not HR reports.

L1
Value Ledger
Total financial cost and return of attrition, hiring, and retention.
L2
Talent Premium
Return on Stars and HiPos above baseline — and liability of Inconsistent Performers below it.
L3
Org Vitals
20 organisational telemetry signals across Leadership, Fear, Middle Layer, Execution, HR Mirror.
L4
Human P&L
HR programme costs, compliance spend, legal exposure — costs that never appear in the P&L but are always paid.
L5
Human Balance Sheet
Institutional knowledge, succession depth, talent concentration risk.
Built for India

Not adapted for India. Built in it.

Every legal citation uses BNS, BNSS, and BSA — the codes that replaced IPC, CrPC, and the Indian Evidence Act on 1 July 2024. Every financial calculation is in INR. Every benchmark reflects Indian private sector practice. Every POSH citation knows the correct name is Internal Complaints Committee.

🇮🇳India-first · BNS / BNSS / BSA · INR · State-specific compliance
The Vision

HR holding leadership accountable — not the other way around.

Every HR professional in India should be able to walk into a boardroom, a difficult performance conversation, or a complex disciplinary situation — and have the intelligence, the legal grounding, and the financial evidence to lead the decision, not just implement it.
