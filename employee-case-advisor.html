⚡ Employee Case Advisor
Tell me what's happening : I'll run the PACT diagnostic and build your Unified Decision Protocol.
Something's landed on your desk.
PACT + PROTOCOL Two layers, always. The PACT diagnostic scans across Process Integrity, Accountability Clarity, Consequence Mapping, and Trust Preservation. The Unified Decision Protocol translates that scan into assessment, legal position, financial exposure, one clear recommendation, and suggested wording for the room — a draft to adapt, not a script to read verbatim.

For a sharper answer, include what you can
  • Who: the roles involved and the employee's years of service
  • What happened, and when: the key events in order, with dates or durations
  • What is already on paper: warnings, notices, emails, policy clauses
  • The decision in front of you: what you must decide, and by when
  • What leadership wants: and where it differs from your read
