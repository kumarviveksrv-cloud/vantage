Common questions
Is there an annual plan? ▾
No. Vantage is monthly only, always. There is no annual lock-in, no discounted upfront payment, no commitment period. You pay month to month and cancel whenever you choose. The retention mechanism is value, not a contract.
What happens to my data if I cancel? ▾
Your MERIDIAN profile, case library, and Humac score data are retained for 30 days after cancellation. You can export your data from the Data & Privacy section before cancelling. After 30 days, data is permanently deleted from Vantage servers.
Can I switch from Core to Consultant? ▾
Yes. Switching tiers takes effect from the next billing cycle. Your existing data — MERIDIAN profile, case history, Humac score — carries over completely. Nothing is lost on upgrade.
How many MERIDIAN profiles does Consultant support? ▾
The Consultant tier is designed for HR practitioners managing multiple client engagements. Each client gets their own fully calibrated MERIDIAN profile. You switch context per session and all six tools recalibrate accordingly.
Is there a free trial? ▾
Vantage is currently in early access with a small group of HR professionals. If you've received an invite, you have full access during this period. Paid subscriptions go live once early access closes. Reach out to the team at virorah.com if you're interested in early access.
Is Vantage only available in India? ▾
Yes, currently. Vantage is built specifically for Indian HR practice — BNS / BNSS / BSA legal citations, INR financials, state-specific compliance, and Indian employment law frameworks. Global expansion is planned. If you're outside India and interested, note your country when reaching out.
