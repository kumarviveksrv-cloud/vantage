Common questions
Is there an annual plan? ▾
No. Vantage is monthly only, always. There is no annual lock-in, no discounted upfront payment, no commitment period. You pay month to month and cancel whenever you choose. The retention mechanism is value, not a contract.
What happens to my data if I cancel? ▾
Cancelling your subscription does not delete your data on its own: your MERIDIAN profile, case library, and Humac score remain in your account. To delete everything, use "Delete Everything Permanently" in Data & Privacy, which starts a 30-day grace period before permanent deletion. You can export your data from Data & Privacy at any time before then.
Is there a free trial? ▾
Yes. Every new account gets a 2-day trial with up to 10 uses across the five working tools (Employee Case Advisor, The Business Case, Policy Advisor, Retention Advisor and Difficult Conversations), with no card required. Whichever limit you reach first ends the trial. ARIA is part of the paid plan, and MERIDIAN, Humac Score, Vantage Record, Standpoint and The Debrief are not part of the trial limit. After that, Vantage is ₹799 per month, billed monthly, and you can cancel anytime.
Is Vantage only available in India? ▾
Yes, currently. Vantage is built specifically for Indian HR practice: BNS / BNSS / BSA legal citations, INR financials, state-specific compliance, and Indian employment law frameworks. Global expansion is planned. If you're outside India and interested, note your country when reaching out.
