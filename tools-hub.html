Where would you like to work today?
Five tools. Each built for a specific moment in your role as an HR professional.
Employee Case Advisor
An employee situation with legal stakes. Get process risk, financial exposure, and exact next steps — calibrated to your context.
The Business Case
Numbers for the room, or an argument for one specific person. Translate people decisions into financial language the business actually hears.
Policy Advisor
Describe a policy gap and get the applicable legal framework — the Acts, the rules, and the specific compliance requirements that apply in your state.
Retention Advisor
Model the real cost of keeping or losing this person. Replacement cost, productivity loss, and the financial case for a counter-offer — built from your numbers.
ARIA · Rehearse
Rehearse before the conversation that matters. Practice the message, the pushback, and the recovery — before you walk into the room.
