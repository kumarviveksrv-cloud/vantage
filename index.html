05 / ARIA

The conversation is the real test.

You don't need another chatbot. You need a room where you can practice before the room that matters.

ARIA is reflective coaching — talking a decision through before or after you make it. Conversation Simulator, above, is where you rehearse the actual exchange with a stakeholder voice.

Manager

"Why can't we just terminate him? We've already waited long enough."

ARIA

"Don't defend the policy yet. Ask what outcome he actually needs. Then separate urgency from the process required to get there."

You

"If closure is the goal, let's make sure the route to closure doesn't create a bigger problem."
