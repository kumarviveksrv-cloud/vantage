The question everyone asks

ChatGPT knows HR.
It doesn't know yours.

You've probably already tried it. Typed your situation into ChatGPT, read a thorough answer, then sat there wondering how any of it applied to your company, your city, your standing orders, your CFO's priorities. That's not a failure of the tool. It's a failure of the category. Generic intelligence cannot solve specific problems. Vantage was built because of that gap.

01
It doesn't know you're in Haryana.
ChatGPT gives the exact same termination answer to an HR Manager in a 50-person Bangalore startup and an HR Director at a 2,000-person Haryana manufacturer operating under certified standing orders. Your state, your industry, your org type, your union status — none of it exists when you ask a general AI. MERIDIAN changes that. Seven parameters calibrate every single output to your exact professional reality before you type a word.
02
It can't tell you what your attrition actually costs.
ChatGPT can tell you attrition is expensive. It cannot tell you your 18% attrition is costing ₹2.4 crore per quarter — calculated against your org size, your industry's replacement cost norms, and your average CTC. Humacity translates every people decision into its financial dimension. Because the only language that moves a CFO is the language of money.
03
It doesn't know what a domestic enquiry is when it actually matters.
PACT, SIGNAL, INFLUENCE, MERIDIAN — purpose-built for the specific realities of Indian HR. The domestic enquiry procedure. The POSH Act's external ICC member requirement. The Model Standing Orders vs certified standing orders distinction that changes your entire disciplinary process.
04
It stops at the answer. We stay with you through the action.
Vantage doesn't give you information. It gives you your next five moves, the exact conversation to practice before you have it, the INFLUENCE Stack for the boardroom, and the financial argument that makes them act. It doesn't stop at the answer. It stays with you until the action is done.
05
Every other HR platform was sold to your company. This one was built for you.
Keka, DarwinBox, Zoho People — your company bought those. You are a user of tools that serve the organisation's interests, not yours. Vantage is the first HR intelligence platform built for the individual HR professional. Your cases. Your frameworks. Your monthly Debrief. Your career.
