01 / LAW

The law — current, cited, and calibrated to you.

India’s 29 central employment laws became 4 Labour Codes on 21 November 2025. The Industrial Disputes Act is gone. The Industrial Relations Code replaced it. The gap between the old laws and the new Codes is where most legal exposure now sits. Vantage runs on what is actually in force today — the specific Code, the specific section, and the exact threshold that applies to your state, your org size, and the case on your desk right now.
