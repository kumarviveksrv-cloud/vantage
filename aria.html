What is ARIA?
ARIA stands for Adaptive Reflective Intelligence for Advancement. She is the voice intelligence layer of Virorah Vantage, designed not to answer HR questions generically, but to think through your specific professional situation with you, in real time, calibrated to your MERIDIAN context.

ARIA is not a search engine. She is a thinking partner. She asks one question at a time, listens to what you say, and builds her understanding of your situation across the conversation. The more you share in this session, the sharper she gets within it. Conversations aren't saved to Virorah's servers unless you explicitly save them, so that understanding starts fresh next time.
How is ARIA different?
Every response ARIA generates knows your state, your industry, your org type, your seniority, and your company's policy guardrails, because your MERIDIAN profile is injected into every prompt. You are not getting a generic HR answer. You are getting an answer calibrated to your professional reality.
The three modes
Explore
Something has landed on your desk and you're not yet sure what kind of problem it is. Talk it through with ARIA. She knows your Humac Score, your case count, and your exposure total, so she isn't starting from zero. She'll help you see the shape of it, and point you to Employee Case Advisor, Retention Advisor, or whichever tool actually fits once that's clear.
Use this when: something just happened and you need to think it through fast, before you know which tool you need.
Reflection
Think out loud about your month: the cases you navigated, the decisions that still sit with you, the patterns you're noticing, the things you'd do differently. ARIA knows your actual case count, your Humac Score trend, and your most recent Debrief, so this isn't generic reflection prompting, it's grounded in what you've actually built on Vantage. She listens, asks one question at a time, and surfaces what's actually underneath what you're saying. It's a structured professional retrospective, not a therapy session.
Use this when: you feel like you've been on autopilot and want to step back and understand what's actually been happening.
Rehearse
You need to have a real conversation and you know it. Pick who you're facing — a direct report, your manager, a skip-level leader — describe what's at stake, and run the conversation before it counts. ARIA plays the other party, calibrated to your MERIDIAN context. After the session, you get a coaching debrief: what landed, what didn't, and one technique to try differently in the real conversation.
Use this when: you have a specific conversation coming up and you want to go in having already said the hard part once.
Using the mic
Tap the mic once to start speaking. ARIA listens continuously, no need to rush. After 5 seconds of silence, it processes what you said. If you have "Review before sending" turned on, your transcript appears in an edit box before it's sent, so you can correct any mishearing. Tap the mic again any time to stop early.
Your privacy
ARIA conversations are processed through Anthropic's Claude API. All content is treated as confidential. Conversations are saved locally to your browser (not to Virorah's servers) unless you explicitly save them. Your MERIDIAN context is included in each prompt to calibrate the response. It is not retained by Anthropic.
