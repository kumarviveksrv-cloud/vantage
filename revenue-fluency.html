Frequently asked

Is this another tool my company has to buy and roll out?

No. Vantage isn't an organisational system at all, and it isn't an HRIS, ATS, or payroll tool either. Those manage the company's people data. Vantage is built for you, the individual HR professional, the way a personal business partner would be: somewhere to think through a hard call, quantify what it actually costs, and keep a record of the decisions you made and why. Nobody asks IT for approval. Nobody else inside the organisation needs to adopt it for you to use it. It's a genuinely new kind of tool for the profession: private, personal, and yours, in a field that has never had anything like it before.

Does my company see what I do on Vantage?

No. Vantage is built for you personally, not licensed to your employer. What you research, the cases you document, the conversations you rehearse with ARIA: none of it is visible to your organisation. Your Vantage Record moves with you if you change jobs; your HRIS never does.

How is this different from just using a general AI chatbot?

A general-purpose large language model, whichever one you use, answers the question you typed, starting from zero every conversation. Vantage is a different kind of thing: a contextual decision system. MERIDIAN calibrates every response to India's current labour codes, your state, your industry, your org size, and your seniority before you type a word. The tools build a case history, quantify financial exposure in INR, and compound in value the longer you use them. Vantage starts from your context, your record, and your professional reality, not a blank page.

Is the legal information current and accurate?

Vantage runs on India's current labour codes, with the verification date shown alongside each legal reference on the page. Labour law changes over time and varies by state. Vantage is a decision aid, not a substitute for your own legal counsel on a specific case.

The numbers you see in the product previews on this page are illustrative examples, built to show you how the calculation works, not live data. Once you're inside Vantage, every number is calculated from the actual details of your own case, with a confidence level attached.

How is my data secured?

Vantage is built on infrastructure that carries enterprise-grade security certifications, even though Virorah as a company does not yet hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 in its own name.

Authentication: Every sign-in goes through Clerk, which is SOC 2 Type II certified. Your credentials are never stored by Vantage directly.

Data storage: Your case records, MERIDIAN profile, and Humac Score are stored in Airtable, which is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Data is hosted on servers in the United States.

API and traffic layer: All traffic between your browser and Vantage's backend runs through Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified.

AI processing: Anthropic, the AI provider powering Vantage's tools, does not use API data to train its models by default. This is documented in their usage policy.

Anonymisation — a two-step process: When you submit a case narrative, your original text first reaches Anthropic's Claude Haiku model, which identifies and replaces person names, company names, and specific locations with role-consistent placeholders. Only the anonymised output then reaches Claude for the actual analysis — your original text reaches Anthropic once, for anonymisation only. This step fails closed: if anonymisation cannot complete safely, the request is blocked rather than sent unredacted.

Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit over TLS. Your case content is never shared with advertisers or used for any purpose outside your account.

Your control: You can export all your data or delete it permanently at any time from within your account.

Where is my data actually stored?

Your case records and profile are stored with Airtable, on servers based in the United States. Your original, unredacted text stays there. Your original text reaches Anthropic's Claude Haiku model once, for the anonymisation step only. Only the anonymised version is then sent to Claude for analysis. You can export everything or delete it permanently, at any time, from within your account.
