Step 2 of 3 — Recommended
Humac Score
Measure your org's human capital.
Your Humac Score is a 0–100 composite that converts how efficiently your organisation turns people investment into financial value. Five weighted pillars. One number you can take into any boardroom.

This is what The Business Case uses to calculate your actual Revenue at Risk — not a sector benchmark, your org's real number. It's also what ARIA draws on when it contextualises your decisions against your actual position.

Takes about 10 minutes. You can do it later — but once you have it, the gap between generic advice and real intelligence closes.
