What is Humacity?
Humacity is the philosophy that every people decision has a financial dimension. It translates HR outcomes into four asset classes — Trust Capital, Culture Yield, Signal Intelligence, and Belonging Premium — creating the organisation's Human P&L. HR is not a support function. It is a P&L operator.
