1. Who We Are: Data Fiduciary

Virorah Private Limited ("Virorah", "we", "us") is the Data Fiduciary for the Virorah Vantage platform under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act). This means we determine the purposes and means of processing your personal data.

You, as the user of the Platform, are the Data Principal under the DPDP Act.

Our registered office is in Gurugram, Haryana, India. Contact: info@virorah.com

4. Anonymisation Before AI Processing

Before any text you write is sent to our AI processing layer (Anthropic's Claude API), the Platform automatically identifies person names, company names, and specific locations, and replaces them with role-consistent placeholders: for example, "Manager A" and "Employee B" rather than real names, inferred from context so the underlying diagnostic still makes sense. This applies across every AI-powered tool on the Platform: the Employee Case Advisor, The Business Case, Policy Advisor, Retention Advisor, Difficult Conversations, and ARIA.

What happens if anonymisation cannot be completed This step fails closed: if the anonymisation process cannot produce a safe result after a brief automatic retry, the Platform blocks that request entirely rather than sending your original text unredacted. You will see an error asking you to try again, not a silent fallback.

For ARIA and Difficult Conversations specifically (both ongoing conversations rather than single submissions), the Platform anonymises each new message as it is sent. Earlier turns in the same conversation are not re-processed on every new message, since they were already anonymised when originally sent.

Separately: your original text, exactly as you typed it, is stored in your own Airtable record and is not shared with any third party beyond what is described in Section 7. Only the anonymised version is sent to Anthropic.

5. Legal Basis for Processing: DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, Virorah processes your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Section 6, DPDP Act 2023): By creating a MERIDIAN profile and using the Platform, you provide free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent to the processing of your personal data as described in this Policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account and data from the Platform.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing necessary to comply with applicable Indian law.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal. To withdraw consent, use the "Delete Everything Permanently" function in the Data & Privacy section of the Platform.

6. Data Storage and International Transfer

Your data is stored using the following infrastructure:

  • Airtable (US servers): Your MERIDIAN profile, case records, and debrief data are stored in Airtable, a cloud database service. Airtable's servers are located in the United States (AWS us-east-1). By using the Platform, you explicitly consent to this cross-border transfer of your personal data to the United States.
  • Browser Storage: Your session data (access status, current-session MERIDIAN profile) is stored in your browser's sessionStorage and localStorage. This data never leaves your device except as described above.
  • Anthropic (US servers): Anonymised text: with person names, company names, and locations replaced with role-consistent placeholders as described in Section 4, is processed by Anthropic's Claude API, with servers in the United States. Under Anthropic's API terms, this data is not used to train their models.

Virorah takes reasonable contractual and technical measures to ensure that cross-border transfers are made only to jurisdictions or entities that provide an adequate level of data protection. However, you should be aware that data protection laws in the United States differ from those in India.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Specifically:

  • MERIDIAN Profile: Retained until you delete it or delete your account
  • Case Records: Retained until you delete individual cases or delete your account
  • Debrief Records: Retained until you delete your account
  • Post-deletion: After you delete your account or all data, records are permanently deleted from Airtable within 30 days. Browser localStorage and sessionStorage data is deleted immediately upon your request or when you clear your browser data.
  • Legal Hold: Where Virorah is required by law to retain certain data, it will be retained for the legally required period and then deleted.

9. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act 2023, here are the rights the Act gives you, and one export we offer anyway:

Right to Access
View all personal data held about you. Available in the Data & Privacy section of the Platform.
Right to Correction
Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data. Update your MERIDIAN profile at any time via onboarding.html.
Right to Erasure
Request permanent deletion of all your data. Use "Delete Everything Permanently" in Data & Privacy. This starts a 30-day grace period, cancelled if you log in before it ends; after that, deletion is permanent and irreversible.
Not a DPDP Right, Offered Anyway
Export all your data in JSON format. Use "Export All My Data" in Data & Privacy at any time.
Right to Withdraw Consent
Withdraw your consent to data processing at any time by deleting your data. Withdrawal does not affect prior processing.
Right to Grievance Redressal
Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer at info@virorah.com. We will respond within 48 hours.

To exercise any of these rights, visit the Data & Privacy section of the Platform or contact us at info@virorah.com.

10. Security

Virorah implements the following technical and organisational security measures:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Access to Airtable data is restricted via API key authentication
  • Text is anonymised: person names, company names, and specific locations replaced with role-consistent placeholders, before it reaches Anthropic's Claude API, across every AI-powered tool on the Platform (Section 4)
  • This step fails closed: if anonymisation cannot be completed safely, the request is blocked rather than sent unredacted
  • Browser sessionStorage is cleared on sign-out

No system is completely secure. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, Virorah will notify affected users as required by the DPDP Act 2023 and report to the Data Protection Board of India within the prescribed timeframe.

11. Cookies and Local Storage

The Platform does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies. The Platform uses:

  • Browser sessionStorage: To maintain your session (access status, current MERIDIAN profile). Cleared when you close your browser or sign out.
  • Browser localStorage and Clerk session storage: Clerk, our authentication provider, manages sign-in session data in your browser to keep you logged in. This is managed by Clerk directly, not by Virorah's own servers.

The Platform does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other third-party tracking technology.

12. Children's Data

The Platform is intended for use by adults (18 years and above) in a professional capacity. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided data to the Platform, please contact us at info@virorah.com and we will delete it immediately.

The date of the most recent revision is indicated at the top of this Policy. Continued use of the Platform after the effective date of a revised Policy constitutes your acceptance of the revised terms.
