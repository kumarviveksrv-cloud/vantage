1. Who We Are — Data Fiduciary

Virorah Private Limited ("Virorah", "we", "us") is the Data Fiduciary for the Virorah Vantage platform under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act). This means we determine the purposes and means of processing your personal data.

You, as the user of the Platform, are the Data Principal under the DPDP Act.

Our registered office is in Gurugram, Haryana, India. Contact: info@virorah.com

2. What We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide and improve the Platform. Here is a complete breakdown:

Data Category Specific Data How Collected Why
Identity & Access Anonymised user ID (randomly generated, stored in browser localStorage) Auto-generated on first access To associate your records with your account without requiring personal identification
MERIDIAN Profile State/region, industry, organisation type, organisation size, HR seniority level, union status, HR function, policy guardrail settings Provided by you during onboarding To calibrate AI outputs to your professional reality
Case Data Anonymised case narratives, case type, PACT assessment results, status, retrospective notes Provided by you when using the ER Case Navigator To build your Case Library and generate your monthly Debrief
Debrief Data AI-generated monthly debrief content, case statistics Generated by the Platform based on your case data To deliver your monthly professional reflection
Usage Data Session data stored in browser sessionStorage (access status, MERIDIAN data for current session) Auto-generated during use To maintain your session without requiring repeated login
Payment Data Subscription tier, payment status (processed entirely by Razorpay) Via Razorpay on subscription To manage your subscription. Virorah does not store card details.

What we do NOT collect: Your real name, email address (unless you contact us), employer name, employee names, or any other directly identifying personal information about you or third parties mentioned in your case narratives.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide calibrated AI assessments, frameworks, and intelligence outputs based on your MERIDIAN profile and inputs
  • Case Library: To store and display your case records for your professional reference
  • Monthly Debrief: To generate your monthly professional reflection from your case data
  • Platform Improvement: Aggregated, anonymised usage patterns (not individual records) may be used to improve the Platform's models and user experience
  • Subscription Management: To manage your subscription, process payments (via Razorpay), and communicate about your account
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable Indian law, including the DPDP Act 2023 and the IT Act 2000

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data to any third party. We do not use your individual case narratives or outputs to train AI models.

4. Anonymisation Before AI Processing

This is one of the most important privacy protections in the Platform. Before any case narrative or situation description is sent to our AI processing layer (powered by Anthropic's Claude API), the Platform automatically anonymises your input:

  • All person names are replaced with Role A, Role B, Role C in order of appearance
  • Company names are replaced with Company X
  • Specific locations (cities, office names) are replaced with Location Y
  • Other identifying details are replaced with generic equivalents
The AI model (Anthropic's Claude) never receives your real name, your employer's name, or any directly identifying information. It receives only anonymised narratives and your MERIDIAN profile parameters (state, industry, org type — none of which identify you personally).

The original, non-anonymised narrative you type is stored only in your personal Airtable record and is never shared with any third party other than as described in Section 7.

5. Legal Basis for Processing — DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, Virorah processes your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Section 6, DPDP Act 2023): By creating a MERIDIAN profile and using the Platform, you provide free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent to the processing of your personal data as described in this Policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account and data from the Platform.
  • Legitimate Use for Contract Performance: Processing necessary to deliver the Platform services you have subscribed to.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing necessary to comply with applicable Indian law.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal. To withdraw consent, use the "Delete All My Data" function in the Data & Privacy section of the Platform.

6. Data Storage and International Transfer

Your data is stored using the following infrastructure:

  • Airtable (US servers): Your MERIDIAN profile, case records, and debrief data are stored in Airtable, a cloud database service. Airtable's servers are located in the United States (AWS us-east-1). By using the Platform, you explicitly consent to this cross-border transfer of your personal data to the United States.
  • Browser Storage: Your session data (access status, current-session MERIDIAN profile) is stored in your browser's sessionStorage and localStorage. This data never leaves your device except as described above.
  • Anthropic (US servers): Anonymised case narratives are processed by Anthropic's Claude API, with servers in the United States. As described in Section 4, only anonymised data is sent to Anthropic. Under Anthropic's API terms, your data is not used to train their models.

Virorah takes reasonable contractual and technical measures to ensure that cross-border transfers are made only to jurisdictions or entities that provide an adequate level of data protection. However, you should be aware that data protection laws in the United States differ from those in India.

7. Third-Party Processors

Virorah shares your data only with the following third-party processors, solely for the purposes described:

Processor Data Shared Purpose Location
Anthropic, Inc. Anonymised case narratives, MERIDIAN parameters (state, industry, org type — no personal identifiers) AI processing and output generation United States
Airtable, Inc. User ID, MERIDIAN profile, case records, debrief records Data storage and retrieval United States
Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. Payment details (card information processed directly by Razorpay; Virorah receives only payment status) Payment processing India

We do not share your data with any other third party, including advertisers, data brokers, analytics companies, or marketing platforms.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Specifically:

  • MERIDIAN Profile: Retained until you delete it or delete your account
  • Case Records: Retained until you delete individual cases or delete your account
  • Debrief Records: Retained until you delete your account
  • Post-deletion: After you delete your account or all data, records are permanently deleted from Airtable within 30 days. Browser localStorage and sessionStorage data is deleted immediately upon your request or when you clear your browser data.
  • Legal Hold: Where Virorah is required by law to retain certain data, it will be retained for the legally required period and then deleted.

9. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the following rights:

Right to Access
View all personal data held about you. Available in the Data & Privacy section of the Platform.
Right to Correction
Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data. Update your MERIDIAN profile at any time via onboarding.html.
Right to Erasure
Delete all your data permanently. Use "Delete All My Data" in Data & Privacy. Deletion is permanent and irreversible.
Right to Portability
Export all your data in JSON format. Use "Export All My Data" in Data & Privacy at any time.
Right to Withdraw Consent
Withdraw your consent to data processing at any time by deleting your data. Withdrawal does not affect prior processing.
Right to Grievance Redressal
Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer at info@virorah.com. We will respond within 48 hours.

To exercise any of these rights, visit the Data & Privacy section of the Platform or contact us at info@virorah.com.

10. Security

Virorah implements the following technical and organisational security measures:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Access to Airtable data is restricted via API key authentication
  • Case narratives are anonymised before leaving your browser and before AI processing
  • No employee personal data or real names are stored on the Platform's servers
  • Browser sessionStorage is cleared on sign-out

No system is completely secure. In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, Virorah will notify affected users as required by the DPDP Act 2023 and report to the Data Protection Board of India within the prescribed timeframe.

11. Cookies and Local Storage

The Platform does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies. The Platform uses:

  • Browser sessionStorage: To maintain your session (access status, current MERIDIAN profile). Cleared when you close your browser or sign out.
  • Browser localStorage: To store your anonymised user ID for data persistence across sessions. You can clear this by using the "Delete All My Data" function.

The Platform does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other third-party tracking technology.

12. Children's Data

The Platform is intended for use by adults (18 years and above) in a professional capacity. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided data to the Platform, please contact us at info@virorah.com and we will delete it immediately.

13. Changes to This Policy

Virorah may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. Where changes are material, we will notify you via the Platform with at least fifteen (15) days' notice before the changes take effect.

The date of the most recent revision is indicated at the top of this Policy. Continued use of the Platform after the effective date of a revised Policy constitutes your acceptance of the revised terms.
